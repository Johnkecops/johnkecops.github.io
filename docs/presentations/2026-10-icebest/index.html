  • Training big AI models require huge amount of energy, only nuclear power plant and renewable sources (hydro, thermal, solar panel, etc) could provide.
  • Indonesia needs to resolve its energy sustainability issue before even thinking to do significant steps in this initiatives.

Appendix: Glossary of Terms

  • Bioindustry: The sector encompassing biotechnology applications in healthcare, pharmaceuticals, and life sciences
  • Machine Learning: AI algorithms that enable computers to learn and improve from experience without explicit programming
  • Drug Discovery: The process of identifying and developing new pharmaceutical compounds for therapeutic use
  • Biomarkers: Biological indicators used to measure disease presence, progression, or treatment response
  • Precision Medicine: Medical approach that customizes treatment based on individual patient characteristics
  • QSAR: Quantitative Structure-Activity Relationship - mathematical models predicting biological activity from molecular structure
  • Clinical Trials: Research studies testing safety and efficacy of medical treatments in human subjects
  • Regulatory Approval: Government authorization required before marketing pharmaceutical products
  • Sustainable Development: Meeting present needs without compromising future generations' ability to meet their needs
